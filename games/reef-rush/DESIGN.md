# Reef Rush

Swim a reef course against three rivals and reach the finish gate first. Grab items from clams, hold up to three, and fire the one you pick.

This is the repo's first full game and its reference layout: logic in feature folders under `src/logic/`, one orchestrating `world.ts`, engine seams (`art/index.ts`, `art/font.ts`, `audio/index.ts`, `themes/index.ts`) that later work swaps behind.

## Courses and themes

A course is data in a registry: `logic/courses/<id>.ts` holds its level, the medium it is raced in (`swim`), its theme, its race song, whether it is listed on course select, its par time and any tuning, and `logic/courses/index.ts` (`COURSES`, `LISTED_COURSES`, `DEFAULT_COURSE`, `courseById`) is the only file that imports a course file. Sunlit Shallows is the one shipped course. How to add a course or a medium is in `COURSES.md`.

A theme is how a course looks, in the engine: `engine/themes/<id>.ts` built with `defineTheme` (types in `themes/types.ts`) registers its own textures and animations at boot and says how the views draw it: terrain tiles per cell and per legend glyph art, flowing cells (kelp, currents), the finish gate and debris chunk, backdrop bands and painted layers, caustics, light rays and motes (each optional), particle physics (how things rise, fall, drift and splash), hazard skins, the dark-zone tint, the contrast bands sprites and text must stand off, and any extra race layers. `THEMES` and `themeFor(course)` are in `themes/index.ts`. The ocean theme (`themes/ocean.ts`, art, keys and colours in `themes/ocean/`) keeps the texture key strings from before themes existed. Racers, pickups, power-ups, the HUD and the UI are global art (`art/keys.ts`) and look the same on every course; adding a theme never edits `art/keys.ts` or `art/index.ts`. A course whose theme or song the engine doesn't have, or whose legend names art its theme has no tile for, fails `themes/course-assets.test.ts`, and the course a session starts on is checked at boot (it throws naming the course and what is missing). The race song is the course's (`Course.song`).

Best times are kept per course and per level (save v6 `bests[courseId][level]`), and the save remembers the last listed course raced (`lastCourse`). An unlisted course (tests, previews, reachable only by `?course=`) never gets a best, never shows NEW BEST and is never the last course.

## Core loop

Pick a course (only once more than one is listed), pick a racer, then a 3-2-1 countdown and the race. Swim with momentum, dash for bursts of speed, fire items, ride currents, avoid kelp drag. Finish ahead of three rivals drawn from the rest of the cast. Results show place and time; the best finish on each course and level is saved.

## Controls

| Action                     | Keyboard          | Touch |
| -------------------------- | ----------------- | ----- |
| swim                       | Arrow keys, WASD  | d-pad |
| fire the selected item, confirm | Space, Z, Enter | A |
| menu up/down               | Up/Down, W/S      | d-pad |
| dash, back                 | X, Shift          | B     |
| previous item              | Q                 |       |
| next item                  | E, Tab            | `⇄`   |

Fire (A on touch, Space on a keyboard) uses the item in the selected slot, the one framed in gold on the HUD, and spends one of its charges. Q and E (or Tab, or `⇄` on touch) move the frame to the previous or next held item; pressing next and fire on one tick fires the newly selected item. The dash is the logic's kick, called dash on screen. It runs off a meter of two charges (the pips beside the item well): a dash spends one, and each comes back 1.5 s later (`powerups/dash-meter.ts`), so a dash is saved for a gap, a latch or a squid rather than pressed all race. Rivals use the same meter. How to Play shows it in pictures, and on the first race and every Easy race a hint prompts the first fire and then the first swap.

On course select up and down move the cursor, fire picks the course and dash goes back to the title. On the select screen the arrows (d-pad) move around the grid, fire races as the highlighted character, and dash goes back (to course select when it is shown, else the title). On How to Play, fire or right turns to the next page and dash or left to the previous one. Left and right stop at the first and last page; only dash on the first page and fire on the last page return to the title (with How to play still selected).

## Rules worth knowing

- Tick order and determinism rules are in the header of `src/logic/world.ts`.
- A race must compute the same on every JavaScript engine, because online play will run it on each player's browser from inputs alone. `hashWorld` (`src/logic/hash.ts`) reduces a world's state to one 32-bit number. It is pinned for the top route in `src/logic/top-route.test.ts`, and smoke asserts one number on Chromium, the phone project and WebKit. Logic may not use `**` or the `Math` functions engines round differently (`tools/biome/no-inexact-math.grit`); `src/logic/num.ts` has exact replacements.
- Rivals steer by a distance-to-finish field (`race/flowfield.ts`); placing uses a separate uniform-cost field so places don't flicker.
- The race ends when you finish, 10 s after the third rival finishes (you get TIME UP), or at 5 minutes.
- Rivals swim at par with you (swim force 0.95 to 1.10 of yours), so holding forward keeps you in the race and dashes, items and pearls decide it. Rivals fire items often: on a usable target they fire with a per-tick chance of `itemAggression / reactionTicks` (no minimum delay), and a leader with a Mimic drops it on a chaser. Catch-up scales their swim force by 0.05 per tile of gap, between 0.6x when far ahead of you and 1.25x when far behind (Hard's `DIFFICULTY.hard.rivalCatchUp` in `difficulty-level.ts`). Rivals steer around urchins and jellies they can see coming (`ai/avoid.ts`). They aim attacks at any racer ahead of them, you or another rival, and flash a lantern only at a racer just ahead (`ai/items.ts`). Rival Baron plans through coral on a cheap `ram` flow field, since he breaks it by swimming into it. In a dark zone a rival that can't see (not Selene, no Glow Lantern) wobbles twice as much and replans half as often.
- Difficulty is measured by the `difficulty` Vitest project (`pnpm --filter game-reef-rush test:difficulty`, about 76 s, one file per character per bar so they run in parallel; bots in `race-bots.ts` and `bot-driver.ts`). Hard (`<character>.test.ts`, `<character>-novice.test.ts`, bars in `hard-bars.ts`, 30 seeds): an expert wins at least 18, with close wins at least 18, and its mean finish sits within 0.92-1.08 of par (Sunlit's par is 6720 ticks, 112 s); a novice places 3rd or 4th in at least 18 and finishes within 1.3x the winner in at least 27. The roster is not balanced yet: over seeds 1-100 the Hard expert's win rate runs from Baron 67% to Selene 91%, an open problem recorded in `hard-bars.ts`. Normal (`normal-<character>.test.ts`, 10 seeds): a bot that only swims (no dash, no items) places top 3 in at least 6 (measured 9-10); the expert wins at least 4 more races than it (measured 5-9 more); a novice finishes every race, places top 3 in at least 5 and wins at most 8. Rival items (`rival-items-<character>.test.ts`, Normal, 40 races each): every character's median item uses per race is 5-10 (measured 6-9.5), and `rival-kinds.test.ts` checks every item kind fires. Rivals take two rolls from a clam (the player one), which is what supplies that many uses. Easy (`easy-*.test.ts`): a toddler bot places top 2 in at least 7 of 9, no rival item lands on the player, and the guide halves the time a kid bot spends stuck. `sanity/bars.test.ts` runs in the unit gate: Rudy and Baron on Hard, seeds 9-13, win at least 3 of 5 each and finish near par. Normal's catch-up starts 2 tiles behind the player (`difficulty-level.ts` says why).
- Easy way-finding (`logic/guide.ts`, knobs in `DIFFICULTY[level].guide`, off on Normal and Hard so Hard is unchanged): a player who holds toward the finish and steers only after hitting a wall used to sit against it for many seconds (once about 13 s at the wall at tile 41,4). `guideFor(world, racer)` gives the neighbour tile that lowers the steer field most (the way on, one of 8 offsets) and `world.guide.stuckTicks`, the ticks the player has pushed without reaching a tile closer to the finish by that field. The steering assist: once the player has pushed 0.5 s without progress while nearly at rest, for the next 90 ticks of pushing the part of the rivals' steer aim that is sideways to the stick is added to it (a slide along the wall toward the way on). It never adds speed (swimming normalises the stick), is skipped for a stick held more than 120 degrees away from the way on, and never touches rivals or bots. The arrow (engine, below) shows after 1.5 s stuck. Measured with the stuck-prone kid (`logic/difficulty/kid-bot.ts`: holds right, tries up or down for as long as it keeps getting closer after 1.5 s without progress by the uniform progress field, alternating, kicks every 8 s, never bursts), 40 seeds per character on Easy, stuck time = ticks past the first second of each spell without a step closer by the steer field: Rudy 14.8 -> 2.6 s per race, Gil 19.0 -> 3.1, Onyx 22.7 -> 4.4, Baron 21.1 -> 1.0; mean place 1.70 -> 1.05, 1.82 -> 1.00, 2.10 -> 1.02, 1.40 -> 1.00; DNFs 0, 3, 5, 0 -> 0. `difficulty/easy-guide.test.ts` keeps a 4-seed version of this.
- Slots and humans: a race has four fixed slots (`player`, `tess`, `squib`, `bo`), and `world.humans` lists the ones a person started in (`['player']` unless `createWorld({ humans })` says otherwise). `stepRace(world, inputs, dt)` drives each human slot from its own entry; `stepWorld` is `stepRace` with `{ player: input }`. A human slot with no entry on a tick is taken over by the AI for the rest of the race (`racer.takeover`): it swims with its character's rival profile and attacks like a rival, but keeps a human's status lengths, clam roll and protection. The rules that once meant "the player" mean any human slot (`isHuman`, `isRival` in `logic/racers.ts`); rivals measure catch-up against the leading unfinished human (`race/catch-up.ts`). Easy is single-player in slot 1. The screen follows one slot, the local racer (`engine/local-racer.ts`, `Runtime.localRacer`): camera, HUD, hints, sounds, effects and results are about it.
- Logic never calls `Math.sin`, `cos`, `atan2`, `hypot`, `exp`, `pow` or `log`: engines may approximate them, and a seeded race must replay identically on Chromium and WebKit. Use `src/logic/num.ts`.

## Roster

You race as one of eight characters; the race fills the three rival slots with three of the other seven, drawn by seed from the `roster` random stream (`pickRivals` in `logic/characters.ts`). Characters are data in fixed slots (`player`, `tess`, `squib`, `bo`), so tick order, random streams and tie-breaks never depend on who is picked. Abilities live in `logic/abilities.ts` (numbers in `ABILITY`), called from hooks in the systems they change. Each character also has its own rival profile in `logic/characters.ts`. Each character has its own art (`art/sprites/racers.ts`, portraits and heads in `art/ui/portraits.ts`).

| Character | Look | Ability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rudy | red snapper in a brass diving helmet | Shake it off: stuns and bubble traps last 40% as long |
| Gil | lanky green seahorse | Spring kick: kicks aimed steeply upward push 1.8x as hard |
| Streak | electric-blue sailfish | Wind-up: up to +10% swim force after 3 s swimming straight; a hard turn, stop or hit resets it |
| Onyx | black barracuda with a scarred fin | Ambush: bumping a racer from behind (within 45° of its heading) takes 12% of its speed |
| Nib | cleaner shrimp in goggles | Deep pockets: holds 4 power-ups instead of 3 |
| Baron Barnacle | huge spider crab with a crusted crown | Bulldozer: breaks coral by ramming, bumps with 2x mass, ramps from 0.4 to full force over 1 s after the start or a hit |
| Selene | moon jellyfish with a starlight glow | Moonlight: sees through dark zones, immune to jelly stings |
| Rook | hammerhead shark | Frenzy: +25% swim force for 1.5 s when a racer within 5 tiles is hit |

Dark zones (`Level.dark`) draw a veil (`view/dark-view.ts`, maths in `view/dark-math.ts`) with a light pool around each racer: yours is 64 px and leans ahead as you swim, a Glow Lantern widens it, and urchins and jellies show a faint glint so you can see them coming. Racers who see in the dark (`racerSeesInDark`) get only a light tint. The course has one dark range, columns 530-610 (the last ledge and drop).

## Enemies

Course creatures you beat with a dash bonk (`logic/hazards/enemies/`, numbers in its `tuning.ts`). Touching an enemy in the 12 ticks after a dash, or while boosting, cannonballing or inflated, bonks it; touching it otherwise is a hit. Beaten enemies stay gone and burst into loose pearls. Glyphs: `S` shark, `E` eel, `K` crab.

| Enemy | Does | Beat it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shark | patrols its lane; sees a racer ahead, winds up for 0.5 s (flash and `!`), charges in a straight line, then rests | two bonks (each staggers it), or one Homing Squid; bursts 5 pearls |
| Eel | hides in a wall hole; eyes and bubbles warn for 0.6 s, then it lunges 2 tiles out and back every 2.5 s | one bonk while it is out; 2 pearls |
| Crab | walks the floor, turning at walls and drops | one bonk; 1 pearl |

On Easy a shark never winds up on the player. Rivals steer around enemies and dash into a crab or an out eel when they have a charge. Sunlit Shallows has 2 sharks, 3 eels and 4 crabs after column 60.

## Power-ups

Clams (`?` in the course) open on touch, roll an item weighted by place (leaders get defensive kinds, Glow Lantern and Mimic; trailers get speed and the attack kinds; Undertow only rolls in 3rd or 4th, Cannonball mostly there) and stay shut for 1.5 s. You have three item slots (Nib four), never two of a kind. A new item goes into the first empty slot with its charges, and items never slide between slots. With every slot full you don't open a clam: it stays ready for the next racer that touches it, and you don't grab loose items either. Fire uses only the selected item and spends one charge, then item use cools down for 20 ticks. An item's last charge empties its slot and the selection moves to the next held slot to the right (wrapping). There are no combos: each item does one thing.

An item's passive works only while its slot is selected, except that a Bubble Shield in any slot absorbs a hit.

| Item | Charges | Passive (selected) | Fire |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jet Fins | 3 | +15% speed, faster kick | boost: 0.4 s at 200 px/s along your input |
| Bubble Shield | 1 | absorbs one hit from any slot | fires a bubble that traps a rival |
| Puffer | 2 | doubles bump push | inflate for 1 s: break coral, hit racers, urchin-proof (never shortens a longer inflation) |
| Ink Sac | 3 | | one ink cloud that slows racers behind |
| Glow Lantern | 2 | pulls in pearls within 3 tiles, lights the dark | flash that stuns nearby racers and jellies |
| Cannonball | 1 | | a 1.25 s dash at 250 px/s, inflated and invulnerable the whole way: smashes coral gates and hits racers it touches |
| Homing Squid | 1 (3 in 3rd or 4th) | a squid that locks the nearest racer ahead within 10 tiles and chases it; on contact it wraps them (70% swim force for 1.5 s, three kicks shake it off). A kick just before it arrives dodges it, and it never chases that racer again. It loses its lock in ink and passes harmlessly through an invulnerable racer |
| Riptide | 2 | every current pushes you 1.5x harder | lays an 8-tile current lane along your aim (snapped to the main axis) that pushes anyone in it at 2x a plain current for 3 s |
| Undertow | 1 | | pulls every racer ahead of you back for 1.5 s, harder the bigger their lead; their kicks fight it |
| Mimic | 1 | squids lock on you only from half the usual range | a see-through copy of you swims on along the course for 5 s; a squid that picks it pops it instead of you |

Jet Fins and Cannonball do nothing while a dash is already running (no charge is spent). A hit ends a Jet Fins boost; nothing ends a Cannonball early.

A hit (urchin `*`, jelly `j`, bubble, puffer, flash) pops a Bubble Shield from any slot if you hold one, else knocks the selected item and all its charges off as a loose pickup anyone with room can grab after half a second (they get those charges); with nothing held you're stunned. The selection then moves on as for a used-up item. Every hit gives a second of invulnerability. A stunned, trapped or latched racer doesn't collide with other racers: you swim straight through it and take the place, and the pair keeps passing through until it separates (`race/collide.ts`, `hazards/contacts.ts`). A stunned or trapped racer can still move the selection, but can't fire, dash or swim. Pearls (`o`) work like coins: each adds 1% to your swim force, up to 10, and gives a short boost; the HUD counts them and a chime plays. A hit that lands (not one a shield absorbs) knocks up to three loose, scattered behind you; anyone can grab them at once, you after half a second, and they fade after 10 s (`pickups/pearls.ts`). Rivals fire one slot at a time (`ai/items.ts`): a Puffer or Cannonball first when coral is just ahead, else the first held slot whose kind has a use (a squid when a racer ahead is in lock range, a Riptide when the water ahead is open and a racer is close behind, Undertow when they trail, Mimic when a squid is chasing them, Jet Fins or a Cannonball on a clear path, but rarely).

## Scenes and launch parameters

- `boot`: builds every texture (the global art in `engine/art/index.ts`, the UI kit and every theme's own), then routes.
- `title`: the logo over the swimming cast and the backdrop of the last listed course raced (Sunlit Shallows until then), then five buttons: Race, How to play, Level (EASY, NORMAL or HARD with 1-3 pips, cycling on each press and saved; a new save starts on Easy and every race is built on the saved level), Sound (mute) and Motion (reduced motion), PRESS A on touch screens only, and your best time and place on the current course at the selected level beside the buttons when it has one (it changes as Level cycles). Settings save at once and apply for the session even if the save fails; a fresh save follows the OS reduced-motion preference. What each level changes is in `logic/difficulty-level.ts`.
- `howToPlay`: seven picture pages (swim, and dash on the meter of two, whose pips empty and refill; open clams, hold three, each with its charge digit; fire one item at a time, drawn as the HUD well with the selected slot framed beside the fire control, a trail from that slot to its effect, then the frame moving on; grab pearls, which count up and add speed, and a hit scatters three; dash to bonk enemies: the crab walks and takes one bonk, the shark shows `!`, charges and takes two, the eel lunges from its hole and is bonked while out; the four attack power-ups; dashing to dodge a squid) with a heading, page dots and a NEXT / BACK footer; each page turn emits `howToPlay.page` (1-based) and plays the menu-move sound. It draws the real sprites, animated unless reduced motion is on, the player's saved racer, key caps on a keyboard and the round A and B buttons on touch. Pages live in `engine/view/how-to-play/`, the control glyphs in `view/control-glyphs.ts`, the paging in `engine/how-to-play.ts` and the page number on the runtime. Layout in `UI.md` "How to Play".
- `course`: course select, one row per listed course with its name and your best there on the level in force; the cursor starts on the current course, fire picks it and goes to `select`, dash goes back, and like menus it ignores fire for its first 20 ticks. It is skipped while only one course is listed (today), so Title Race goes straight to `select`. It is drawn by `view/course-view.ts` and holds five courses; layout in `UI.md` "Course select".
- `select`: the eight characters in a 4x2 grid (portrait and short name, the cursor's cell in a gold frame) and a card with the highlighted character's full name, ability and what it does. The cursor starts on your last pick. Fire saves the pick and races; dash goes back. Like menus, it ignores fire for its first 20 ticks. Under the card the highlighted racer swims at 2x, and a footer shows the controls (icons on desktop, A/B letters on touch). Layout in `UI.md` "Select".
- Hints for young players (`view/hints.ts`, drawn by `view/hint-view.ts`): on the first race of a save and on every Easy race, PRESS [fire]! (the Space key cap, or A on touch) shows beside the player when an item first lands (until they fire one), [dash] DASH! shows when a locked-on squid is inside the dodge window (`dodgeTicks(world)`: 12 ticks on Hard, 18 on Normal, 24 on Easy) plus a short lead and the player's dash is ready (PRESS FIRE has no timeout on Easy), SWAP! with the next-item control (E, or `⇄` on touch) shows once a race the first time the player holds two items, until they swap or it has been on screen 4 s (a squid's DASH! pauses it), and the fire glyph pulses beside the HUD's pearl counter once items have sat unused for 5 s. Only one hint shows at a time (DASH!, then PRESS FIRE or the arrow, then SWAP!, then the pulse). On Easy a fifth hint, the guide arrow (`view/guide-view.ts`), points the way on beside the player once they have been stuck 1.5 s; DASH! always outranks it and PRESS FIRE does for the first 4.5 s of being stuck. On touch screens the A button also shows the selected item's icon (`view/fire-button.ts`). `debugSummary()` reports `difficulty` (the setting), `raceDifficulty` (what the race was built on) and `hints` during a race (`hints.guide` is the arrow's tile offset, or null).
- `race`: 3-2-1-GO countdown (the level you are racing on sits under the numeral: its word and 1-3 lit pips, in `debugSummary().levelBanner`), then the race, with FINAL STRETCH and FINISH splashes, or TIME UP if you run out of time. HUD (laid out in `UI.md`; the item well has one slot per inventory place, so four for Nib): place with your portrait (hidden during the countdown; pops up or down on a change), timer, a progress strip with each racer's head, and a well with the dash icon (dim while it recharges) and the item slots: each held item's icon with its charges as one digit, the selected slot in a 1 px gold frame, empty slots a dot. The camera follows you with a deadzone and lookahead. 90 ticks after the race ends you go to results.
- `results`: a podium with medals for the top three, standings with names and times revealed row by row (TIME UP if not finished), your row in sun with your head beside it, a NEW BEST (for this course and level) or BEST NOT SAVED readout, then three buttons (Race again, Character, Title), over the course's own backdrop. While course select is shown, the course's name sits top left.
- Menus ignore fire for their first 20 ticks, so a fire still being pressed from the race can't skip the results.
- Title Race goes to `course` (or `select` while one course is listed); results' Race again races the same course and character again without either select screen, while results' Character opens `course` (or `select`) with the cursor on the current pick, so the next race can run as someone else. B on those screens goes back to results with Character selected when they were opened from results, and to the title when opened from Title.
- `?scene=race` (or `?scene=play`) skips the title and races as the saved pick (Rudy on a fresh save). `?seed=7` changes rival behaviour and which characters you race. Each race is seeded with `seed + races in the save`, so a rematch plays differently while a test run still repeats exactly.
- `?course=<id>` races that course, listed or not (`debugSummary().course` and `.medium` say which); an unknown id logs one warning naming it and races the default (Sunlit Shallows). Without it the session races the save's last listed course, else the default.
- Save version 6: the best finish per course and level (`bests['sunlit-shallows'].hard`, each a time and place or null; a v5 save's bests move under Sunlit Shallows, and a key for a course the game doesn't know is kept and never shown), `lastCourse`, races run, settings (sound, motion, and `difficulty`: easy, normal or hard) and `character`, the last pick. A v2 save loads as Rudy; a v3 save loads on Easy, like a new one.
- `?debug=1` draws hitboxes from logic state and shows the tick and your tile.
- `?stage=<name>` (needs `?debug=1`, or the test harness) puts attack power-ups into the race before its first frame, to review their views without waiting for a roll: `icons-a`, `icons-b`, `icons-c`, `cannonball` (a Cannonball three tiles before the first coral gate), `squid-flight`, `squid-lockon`, `squid-lockon-far`, `squid-latched`, `riptide`, `decoy`, `undertow`, `dark`, `guide`, `enemies` (a crab 3 tiles ahead on the floor walking away and a shark about 6 ahead, rivals moved clear), `enemy-modes` (every enemy kind in every mode, still, for art review) (`engine/debug-stage.ts`). A staged race never saves a result. Without the gate the parameter is ignored.
- `?local=<slot>` (needs `?debug=1`, even in the test harness) races with `<slot>` as the only human: the keyboard drives it, the screen follows it, and the AI swims slot 1. `debugSummary()` then describes that racer and adds `local: '<slot>'` (also for `?local=player`), and a `?stage=` is set up around it. An unknown slot logs one warning naming it and races as slot 1, saved as usual. On an Easy save a slot other than `player` races on Normal, with one warning. A `?local=` race never saves a result. Checked in `tests/local-racer.spec.ts`.
- Attack power-up warnings: a squid locked on you shows an edge arrow, a LOCKED ON! caption and a rising ping (at most once per 1.5 s); a latched squid shows DASH! beside you (PRESS B on touch). Details in `UI.md`.

## Engine layout

`engine/game.ts` wires Phaser to the shell. `engine/runtime.ts` is the only place logic ticks (`game.ts` feeds its clock once per Phaser step): it reads input, steps the world or the menu, drains each tick's events once and hands them to audio and to views that listen. Scenes in `engine/scenes/` build views from `engine/view/` and sync them every frame. Screens follow `UI.md` (palette, type scale, layout grid, safe zones, motion). The canvas fills the window at a whole-number zoom (`engine/stage.ts`); a resize that changes the view size rebuilds the screen showing, keeping the runtime's menu cursor, page and race. Text goes only through `art/font.ts` `createLabel`; global textures through `art/keys.ts`, a course's own through its theme (`themeFor(course)`).

## Art and audio

Every sprite is palette-indexed pixel text, global art in `engine/art/sprites/` and a theme's in `engine/themes/<id>/sprites/`, drawn at boot by `art/pixel.ts` (at most 4 colours per frame; each racer's main colour holds 3:1 contrast against every theme's contrast bands, and at least a quarter of every racer frame's pixels do, spread over most of its height, enforced in `sprites.test.ts`; the ocean urchin holds 3:1 against the ocean's bands in `themes/ocean.test.ts`). The UI kit (fonts, panels, icons, portraits, logo) lives in `engine/art/ui/`. Sound is a four-voice chip synth (`audio/synth.ts`, `sequencer.ts`); songs are written in a small text notation (`audio/notation.ts`, songs in `audio/songs/`) and parsed at load, so a malformed song fails tests. The race theme speeds up for the final stretch.

## Effects

`engine/fx/` turns logic events into particles and camera shake; racer squash, invulnerability flicker and the 2x Puffer live in the racer and power-up views. How bubbles rise, debris sinks and ink and droplets drift comes from the course theme's particle physics. Effects run per logic tick from events, use their own cosmetic rng (never `world.rngs`), and never change logic state. Shake only ever moves the camera for the player's own hits, shield pops (lighter) and cannonball impacts, capped at 4 px. Reduced motion turns shake off, holds particles still, swaps the flicker for a steady half alpha, drops squash and the place flash, and shows the lantern ring at full reach.

## The course

About 90 s for a good player, 616 columns in five sections: the reef (columns 0-94, the opening the power-up scenarios rely on); the current highway (95-246: a current tunnel up top or a kelp bed with clams below, then a floor-current hall); the jelly field (247-364: jellies bobbing high and low in step); the urchin gauntlet (365-466: an urchin-lined tube or clean roads over and under, with a clam pair at its exit); and the coral gate and final drop (467-615: smash the 2-tile gate with a Puffer or climb over it, then ride a downward current to the finish at column 611).

## Course format

`countdown-holds-racers`, `race-to-finish`, `results-after-finish`, `results-ignores-early-kick`, `dnf-results`, `race-again`, `change-character`, `results-to-title`, `title-best`, `clam-stack`, `hit-drops-selected`, `cannonball-item`, `enemy-bonk`, `audio-plays`, `settings-toggle`, `pick-character`, `how-to-play`, `difficulty-setting`, `first-race-hint`, `countdown-level`, `title-best-per-level`. The top route most share is `TOP_ROUTE` in `scenarios/kit.ts`, a recorded run on seed 1 on Hard with no dashes or items that finishes the whole course, over the coral gate rather than through it; any change to the course, its enemies or Hard's rival tuning means re-recording it (the v2 recordings steer along `Medium.aimFrom` snapped to eight directions). A fresh save races on Easy, so scenarios that depend on Hard's speeds or clam rolls start from `HARD_SAVE` and swim `TITLE_TO_RACE` first (both in `kit.ts`). `src/logic/top-route.test.ts` replays it in logic and fails if it ends less than 300 ticks before the DNF cutoff, so a break shows in `check`, not only in smoke. The squid lock-on warning is checked in `tests/attack-warning.spec.ts` from a staged squid (`?stage=`), since no fixed seed's route reliably draws one; the hints on Normal, Easy and a squid lock-on, and the A button's item icon on touch, are in `tests/hints.spec.ts` the same way, and the guide arrow (Easy shows it, Normal does not, it clears once the player swims the way it points) in `tests/guide.spec.ts` from the `?stage=guide` stage. `?course=` is checked in `tests/course.spec.ts`, since scenarios can't pass a query. Idle scenarios wait `IDLE_TO_RESULTS`, which the race limit guarantees. Item scenarios (`cannonball-item`, `hit-drops-selected`) start from a debug stage rather than a seed's clam rolls.
