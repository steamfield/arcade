## Spec

Rush Racers gains a fourth world, Farside: a race through an asteroid belt on the far side of a moon, flown on thrusters in zero gravity. Nothing slows you down in a vacuum, so thrust is for changing your velocity: hold a direction to burn, let go and you keep drifting, burn the other way to brake. A dash is a burn whose speed you keep for a second or two, until thin drag, a wall or a dust cloud takes it. Walls bounce you instead of stopping you. Planets and moons pull you toward them, so the fast line through a bend is a swing around a planet, and a black hole swallows anyone who gets too close and puts them back a second later. Eight new racers fly on the existing ability slots, on the listed course Slingshot Alley, which passes the same difficulty bars as the other listed courses.

In scope (the user gave full creative freedom on 2026-11-23; these are the design's decisions):
- The drift medium (`drift`): thrust, coasting, braking by counter-thrust, a cruise speed thrust can't push past, speed over cruise that lasts, wall bounces, and the dash as a burn along the stick on both axes.
- Space terrain on the shared cells: asteroid rock, ice that breaks (coral), nebula dust that slows (kelp), solar wind streams that push (currents), a dark stretch in a moon's shadow.
- Gravity wells: planets and moons that pull racers toward their centre, and black holes that swallow a racer inside their horizon and respawn it about 1 s later where it last flew free, at rest and briefly invulnerable.
- Easy: stabilisers brake a racer whose stick is idle, and wells pull less.
- A full world like Thornwood: eight racers with art, Farside names and art for the shared items, pickups, enemies and hazards, a space theme, its own song, the medium's How to Play page, and one listed course that passes the bars on Easy, Normal and Hard.

Out of scope: wormholes or any teleport, moving planets, fuel or oxygen meters, landing or walking on planets, gravity on anything but racers (projectiles, loose items, pearls, enemies and the Mimic decoy ignore wells), open map edges, new items, new enemies, a new button, changes under `packages/`, per-world character picks in the save, re-recording the landing page footage.

- Drift numbers (`DRIFT_TUNING`, px and seconds). `force` 260 is thrust acceleration. `drag` 16 px/s² is a constant, small deceleration everywhere (thin dust): it is why a coasting racer loses a little speed and why `dragMul` shows. `maxSpeed` 210 is the hard cap on speed on both axes. `kick` 90 is the speed a dash adds along the stick (or facing). In `extra`: `cruise` 115 (the speed thrust alone reaches, times `1 + (forceMul - 1) * 0.5`); `overDrag` 44 px/s² added to drag while the racer is over its cruise, so a 90 px/s dash is gone in 1.5 s, which is the dash meter's recharge (`DASH_METER.rechargeTicks` 90): dashing on every recharge down a straight averages about cruise + 45 and never sits at `maxSpeed`, and an Ion Thrusters or Comet dash (200 and 250 px/s held) is back to cruise about 1.5 s after it ends; `idleBrake` 8 px/s² added to drag while the stick is idle; `bounce` 0.5 (share of the speed into a wall that comes back); `bounceMinSpeed` 40 px/s (slower impacts still bounce but emit nothing); `dustCruiseMul` 0.6 and `dustDrag` 150 px/s² in nebula dust; `windForce` 200 px/s² in a solar wind cell. `byDifficulty.easy = { idleBrake: 10 }`. Phase 2 adds `wellPull` 1 (multiplies every well's pull; Easy 0.6), `respawnTicks` 60 and `respawnInvulnTicks` 60. Lanes may change values, never keys.
- Thrust never adds speed past the larger of the racer's current speed and its cruise: it can turn a racer that is over cruise without costing more than drag does, and can always slow it. Wind, wells and dashes can take a racer over cruise, up to `maxSpeed`. So `forceMul` scales acceleration and cruise, `kickMul` the dash, `kickCooldownTicks` gates the dash, `dragMul` scales all drag (thin dust, over-cruise drag, idle brake, nebula dust).
- A stunned racer keeps drifting, since nothing stops it; a trapped one floats still (`trappedDrift` 0) and a dropped item stays where it dropped (`looseDrift` 0).
- Effects: `dashAxes: 'both'`, `stompBounce: null`, `solidClams: false`, `pitBelow: false`, `allowsLedges: false`, `assistAxes: 'both'`; `racerCollision: 'solid'`. Kick rules: a dash dodges a Homing Probe, shakes a latch and counters a Tractor Beam. Gil's slot: a dash aimed steeply up or down (`|aim.y| >= ABILITY.springKick.minUp`) has its vertical part 1.8x as strong, through the existing `kickImpulse`.
- A bounce reverses the velocity component a tile stopped, times `bounce`. It applies to every racer in play except one an item dash is driving (`status.dashing > 0`): `driveDash` rewrites that racer's velocity every tick, so it would bounce and report on each of them; it is stopped by the wall like any swimmer and bounces again once the dash ends. `racer.bounced` carries the axis the tile stopped it on (the harder one in a corner) so the view can squash against the wall. Racers bouncing off each other stay the generic separation's job.
- A Tractor Beam (Undertow) is harsher here than in water, because nothing damps what it adds: at full strength (360 px/s² against 260 of thrust for 1.5 s) a racer burning forward ends up near 150 px/s backward. Accepted for now, with a test that it is back at 0.8 cruise within 1.2 s and the dash countering it as elsewhere; if phase 3's bars show it deciding races, the fix is a generic `MediumEffects` strength for the pull in its own scaffold commit. The latch's sideways drift leaves at most about 29 px/s, which drag and the pilot remove; nothing is done about it.
- Wells (phase 2) are level data: `LevelDef.wells: [{ col, row, reach, pull, horizon? }]`, centred on a tile, `reach` and `horizon` in tiles, `pull` in px/s². The pull on a racer is toward the centre with strength `pull * wellPull * (1 - d / reach)` inside `reach` and zero outside; pulls from overlapping wells add. A planet's solid body is drawn in the ASCII as rock. A well with a `horizon` is a black hole: a racer whose centre is within it is swallowed.
- A swallowed racer goes through the shared fall-out timer (`media/fall-out.ts`): `racer.fell`, parked at rest for `respawnTicks`, then `racer.respawned` at its last free spot, the last place its centre was outside every black hole's reach, with `respawnInvulnTicks` of invulnerability. Invulnerable, boosting and cannonballing racers are swallowed too.
- Slingshot Alley is about the length of Bramble Run (par measured as COURSES.md says), with a dark stretch in a moon's shadow, at least three planet swings, two black holes off the main line beside a shortcut, an ice gate, a dust field, a solar wind lane, and every enemy kind.
- The sfx pin over Sunlit must not change. New sfx (phase 4): bounce, swallowed, respawned in space.

Error model: logic stays total over valid state and throws only for programmer errors and bad course data. Course data errors throw `LevelError` with row and col: anything `validateLevel` reports (a start that can't reach the finish without breaking ice), and from phase 2 a well centred off the map, a `reach`, `pull` or `horizon` that isn't positive, a `horizon` not smaller than its `reach`, a start tile inside a black hole's reach, and any well in a level raced in a medium whose `effects.gravityWells` is false. Drift's accessors throw `Error` naming the kind they were handed, like every medium's. A drift tuning missing an extra throws naming the key. A course naming an unknown world throws naming course and world. A world skin or theme missing art for anything the course uses fails `skins.test.ts`, `course-assets.test.ts` and boot, naming the world or course and the key.

Test cases (the scaffold writes them; lanes treat them as read-only):
- Thrust: holding right from rest gains `force * dt` a tick and settles at cruise (within `drag * dt`) inside 1 s; it never passes cruise by thrust alone; `forceMul` 1.4 raises cruise by 20 %.
- Coast: a racer at cruise that lets go is still above 0.7 cruise 1 s later on Hard and Normal (it loses `(drag + idleBrake) * dt` a tick), and keeps its direction; one drifting up-right keeps both components.
- Brake: holding left while drifting right at cruise stops the rightward motion in under 0.6 s, then builds leftward speed.
- Turn: holding up while drifting right at cruise leaves speed at most cruise every tick and velocity pointing more up than right within 1 s.
- Over cruise: a racer set to 180 px/s rightward holding right never gains speed and loses `drag + overDrag` a second; from `maxSpeed` it is back at cruise, and no slower, within 2 s; holding up it turns without its speed dropping faster than that; holding left it slows at `force`.
- Dash: with the cooldown at 0, a dash adds `kick * kickMul` along the stick (along facing with no stick), on either axis, emits `racer.kicked`, 0.5 s later the racer is still more than `kick * 0.6` over cruise and 1 s later more than `kick * 0.25`; dashing every 90 ticks down a straight averages more than `kick * 0.3` over cruise and under 0.85 `maxSpeed`, dipping back near cruise between dashes; `kickCooldownTicks` gates the next one; Gil's slot dashing straight up or straight down gains 1.8x, dashing level 1x, every other slot 1x.
- Cap: speed never passes `maxSpeed` with forceMul 6 and the dash held, nor with wind behind.
- Drag: `dragMul` 2 doubles what a coasting racer loses.
- Dust: in a kelp cell cruise is `cruise * dustCruiseMul` and a racer entering at cruise is down to it within 0.5 s.
- Solar wind: a current cell pushes along its arrow at `windForce` and can carry a racer over cruise; `sampleDriftEnvironment` reads wind from a current cell and dust from a kelp cell under the racer's centre (an Ion Stream lane comes through `feltCurrent`, as for a swimmer).

#### Lane a brief

- Goal: the drift medium's physics: a racer that burns, coasts, brakes, dashes and bounces as the spec says, replacing the scaffold's crude baseline.
- Interface: `applyDriftIntent(racer, intent, env, tuning, mods, events, dt)` and `createDriftMotion(body)` in `physics.ts` (the `Medium.applyIntent` and `createMotion` members); `sampleDriftEnvironment(world, racer): DriftEnv` in `env.ts`; `afterDriftMove(world, racer, contacts, events)` in `bounce.ts` (the `Medium.afterMove` member), reading `contacts.lost`; `DRIFT_TUNING`, `DRIFT_EFFECTS`, `DRIFT_KICK_RULES` and `driftExtras(tuning)` in `tuning.ts`; `DriftMotion`, `DriftEnv`, `driftMotion`, `driftEnv` in `data.ts`. `index.ts` already wires these names; don't rename them.
- Behavior: every line of the Thrust, Coast, Brake, Turn, Over cruise, Dash, Cap, Drag, Dust, Solar wind, Easy, Bounce and Rules test cases above. Order within a tick: facing from the stick; thrust (velocity plus `force * forceMul * dt` along the unit stick, then scaled back to the larger of the speed before thrust and the racer's cruise if it ended above that); wind; drag (a constant deceleration along the velocity, never reversing it: `drag * dragMul`, plus `overDrag * dragMul` while the speed is over the racer's cruise and never taking it below cruise on its own, plus `idleBrake * dragMul` with an idle stick, plus `dustDrag * dragMul` in dust); the dash (through `kickImpulse(racer, aim, DRIFT_KICK_RULES)`); the cap at `maxSpeed`. Cruise is `cruise * (1 + (forceMul - 1) * 0.5)`, times `dustCruiseMul` in dust. The bounce sets the stopped component to `-lost * bounce` on each axis `contacts.lost` is non-zero and emits `racer.bounced` with the length of `lost` and the axis of its larger component when that length is at least `bounceMinSpeed`; a racer with `status.dashing > 0` is left as `moveBody` stopped it. Read every number from the `tuning` handed in, through `driftExtras`.
- Decisions already made: the Assumptions' numbers are starting values; change values if a test case can't hold, never keys, and say which. Keep `drag` between 8 and 30: conformance's open-space progress bar is `0.5 * (force / drag) * 1.5 s`, written for linear drag, and a `drag` near 1 would put it out of cruise's reach. The scaffold's `physics.ts` already has the thrust limit and `overDrag`; the rest of the tick is yours. Logic may use only the `Math` members the lint allows (`sqrt`, `abs`, `min`, `max`, `floor`; `num.ts` has `length`, `sin`, `cos`); no `**`.
- Out of scope: gravity wells, black holes and respawns (phase 2), any pilot or nav change, new events, sound, a fuel or heat limit on thrust, rotation or a facing angle beyond the existing left/right `facing`.
- Done when: `pnpm arcade test rush-racers src/logic/media/drift/physics.test.ts src/logic/media/drift/bounce.test.ts src/logic/conformance/medium.test.ts src/logic/race/move-contacts.test.ts` and `pnpm arcade check rush-racers --skip-tests` pass.

The target speed is the larger of cruise and the speed it already has along its aim, so it keeps what a dash, a wind lane or (phase 2) a planet gave it instead of burning back to cruise, unless open space along its velocity runs out: it must be able to stop before turning into a wall, so cap the target at the speed it can shed with `force` over the free distance ahead (`sqrt(2 * force * distance)`), measured along its velocity up to 8 tiles. Wobble (a bounded walk on the aim angle from `draws.wobble`, bound `profile.wobble`, doubled in a dark zone it can't see in), replans every `profile.reactionTicks`, hazard avoidance (`avoidHazards`), the smash and ram field choice and the dash reserve work as in `swim/pilot.ts`. It dashes when its dash is ready, the roll passes `profile.kickSkill`, its velocity is within about 37 degrees of its aim and the way stays straight for 6 tiles. The player's expert bot in the difficulty bars is this pilot, so a pilot that wastes dashes fails phase 3. Recovery: below 8 px/s for 30 racing ticks, or on `forceRecover`, it pushes sideways to its aim for 20 ticks; a stunned or trapped rival resets the count. It reads only the draws it is handed.
